WHAT IS ICT?

Information and Communications Technology (ICT) combines hardware, software, and networks to enable the digital processing, storage, and sharing of information worldwide. It forms the backbone of modern society, powering high-speed internet, mobile communication, and cloud computing.

ICT drives global connectivity, e-commerce, and remote learning, making information instantly accessible and streamlining business operations. However, it also brings challenges, including cybersecurity threats, privacy concerns, digital fatigue, and the digital divide—where unequal access to technology leaves some communities behind. Ultimately, ICT is a powerful engine for economic and social growth that requires responsible management to ensure equal access and security.
